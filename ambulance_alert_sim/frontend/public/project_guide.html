1. Executive Summary & Mathematical Model

In dense urban traffic corridors, emergency response vehicles experience severe transit delays due to late civilian driver awareness. Siren cues afford minimal reaction distance (<100m). This project models and simulates a 1D corridor of $N = 20$ discrete segments where advance alerts warn downstream drivers, dynamically reducing effective traffic congestion and restoring ambulance speed.

Mathematical Formulation

• Corridor Discretization: Divided into $N = 20$ segments.
• Base Traffic Density: Linear ramp $\rho_{\text{base}} \in [0.2, 0.8]$.
• Speed-Density Governing Law:
$$v(\rho) = v_{\max} \cdot (1 - \rho)$$ where $v_{\max} = 1.0$ segment per time step and $\rho \in [0, 1]$.

Advance-Alert Mechanism

• Alert Horizon ($H$): Lookahead segment reach ($H=3$).
• Driver Cooperation Factor ($\alpha \in [0, 1]$): Yield compliance ($\alpha=0.5$).
• Effective Density Formulation:
$$\rho_{\text{eff}}(i) = \rho_{\text{base}}(i) \cdot (1 - \alpha)$$ $\forall \; i \in [pos + 1, \; \min(pos + H + 1, N)]$.

2. Flaws in Initial Blueprint vs. Corrected Implementation

Fault Identified Preliminary Buggy Behavior Corrected Architecture Logic
Kinematic Lookahead Decoupling Lookahead segment clearing modified indices ahead, but speed evaluated rho_eff[idx] (current segment), giving zero time savings. Ambulance step displacement computes speed based on the upcoming segment rho_eff[next_idx], physically capturing early alert benefits.
Truncated Syntax & Scope Errors Core statements in main.py and experiments.py were truncated in preliminary guides. Complete syntactic rebuild across all components with automated unit test coverage.
Boundary & Array Edge Clipping Float-to-integer cast anomalies caused segment boundary index errors on float horizons. Safe integer casting and boundary guards min(idx + 1, N - 1) prevent array out-of-bounds.

4. Automated Unit Testing Suite: test_simulator.py

An automated invariant suite executed via Pytest ensures mathematical correctness and eliminates regressions:

Key Invariants Tested
  • test_density(): Corridor length equals $N$ and densities are within $[0, 1]$.
  • test_alert_reduces_future_density(): Proves clearing occurs strictly in horizon window.
  • test_speed(): Tests boundary speeds at densities $0.0$, $1.0$, and clipping.
  • test_simulation_finishes(): Guarantees simulation reaches termination boundary $T_{max}$.
  • test_alert_is_not_worse_in_this_model(): Invariant: $t_{\text{alert}} \le t_{\text{baseline}}$.
Edge Case Defenses
  • test_parameter_validation_errors(): Rejects $H < 0$, $\alpha < 0$, $\alpha > 1$.
  • test_float_horizon_safe(): Handles float inputs ($H=3.0$) safely.
  • test_zero_horizon_equals_no_alert(): Confirms $H=0$ matches baseline.
  • test_zero_alpha_equals_no_alert(): Confirms $\alpha=0.0$ matches baseline.
  • test_short_density_array_raises(): Validates array length boundary protection.

6. Viva Voce Defense Dossier (Key Talking Points)

Core Concept & Results

• Core Concept: Audible sirens offer late reaction cues (<100m). Advance digital alerts communicate upstream electronically, enabling drivers to yield before the ambulance arrives.
• Experimental Finding: Early warning saves 19 time steps (42.22% reduction) under typical baseline parameters ($H=3, \alpha=0.5$).
• Horizon Behavior: Once advance warning is provided ($H \ge 1$), upcoming bottleneck segments are cleared proactively.

Limitations & Future Roadmap

• Limitations: Synthetic 1D corridor without multi-lane maneuvers, merging ramps, or traffic light preemption cycles; deterministic compliance modeling.
• Real-World Channels: Integration with navigation apps (Google Maps, Waze), overhead variable message signage (VMS), and cellular geo-broadcasting.
• Future Work: Interfacing with microscopic simulators like SUMO or CORSIM using real municipal road network GIS feeds.
